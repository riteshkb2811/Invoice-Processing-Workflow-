# GST Invoice Guidelines

## Purpose
To define basic GST-related invoice validation requirements for the sample invoice processing system.

## Required Information
- Supplier name and address
- Supplier GSTIN, where applicable
- Invoice number
- Invoice date
- Recipient details
- GST rate and tax amount, where applicable
- Total invoice amount

## Validation Rules
- GSTIN should follow the expected format.
- GST amounts should be consistent with the taxable amount and applicable tax rates.
- CGST and SGST may apply to intra-state transactions.
- IGST may apply to inter-state transactions.
- Missing GST information should trigger manual review when required.

## Exception Handling
Invoices with invalid GSTIN, missing tax details or inconsistent calculations must be flagged for review.

Note: Actual GST applicability and rates must be verified against official regulations.
