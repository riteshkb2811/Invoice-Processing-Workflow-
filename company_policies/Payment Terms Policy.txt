# Payment Terms Policy

## Purpose
To define payment deadlines for company invoices.

## Standard Payment Terms
- Standard vendors: Net 30 days.
- Premium vendors: Net 15 days.
- Government vendors: Net 45 days.

## Payment Rules
- Payment deadlines should be calculated using the applicable invoice date or contractual terms.
- Vendor-specific agreements take precedence over standard payment terms.
- Early payment discounts must be verified.
- Overdue invoices should be flagged for Finance review.

## Required Information
- Invoice date
- Vendor payment terms
- Payment due date
- Invoice amount

## Exception Handling
Invoices with missing payment terms should be sent to the Finance Department.
