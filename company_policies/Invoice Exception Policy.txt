# Invoice Exception Handling Policy

## Purpose
To define how invoices with errors or missing information should be handled.

## Exception Types

### Missing Information
Invoices missing invoice number, vendor name, invoice date or amount must be sent for manual review.

### Duplicate Invoice
Invoices with an existing invoice number and vendor combination should be flagged as potential duplicates.

### Invalid Vendor
Invoices from unregistered or inactive vendors should be routed to Vendor Management.

### Missing Purchase Order
Invoices requiring a PO but missing one should be sent to Procurement.

### Amount Mismatch
Invoices with inconsistent totals or PO amounts should be flagged for Finance review.

### Invalid GST Information
Invoices with missing or inconsistent GST details should be sent for manual review.

## Exception Processing
- Do not automatically approve invoices with unresolved exceptions.
- Record the exception reason.
- Assign the appropriate department.
- Update invoice status after review.

## Possible Statuses
- Pending Review
- Rejected
- Approved
- Pending Procurement
- Pending Finance Review
