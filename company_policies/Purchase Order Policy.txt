# Purchase Order Policy

## Purpose
To ensure invoices are consistent with approved purchase orders.

## Requirements
- Purchase invoices should contain a valid PO number when required.
- The vendor should match the vendor mentioned in the PO.
- Invoice amounts should be checked against PO amounts.
- Purchase quantities should match the approved purchase order.

## Validation Rules
- Missing PO number: Send to Procurement Department.
- Invalid PO number: Send for manual review.
- Invoice amount exceeding PO amount: Flag for review.
- Vendor mismatch: Reject automatic processing.

## Routing
Invoices requiring PO verification should be routed to the Procurement Department.
