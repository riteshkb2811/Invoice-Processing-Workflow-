# Expense Policy

## Purpose
To define company expense approval requirements.

## Eligible Expenses
- Office supplies
- Software subscriptions
- Business travel
- Equipment purchases
- Professional services

## Expense Requirements
- Every expense must have a valid business purpose.
- Supporting invoices or receipts must be provided.
- Expenses should be associated with the appropriate department.
- Expenses must comply with company spending limits.

## Restricted Expenses
- Personal purchases
- Unauthorized subscriptions
- Unapproved purchases
- Expenses without supporting documentation

## Routing
- Department-related expenses → Department Manager
- Financial exceptions → Finance Manager
- Restricted or suspicious expenses → Manual Review
