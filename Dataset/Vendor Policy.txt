# Vendor Policy

## Purpose
To define vendor validation requirements.

## Vendor Requirements
- Every vendor should have a unique vendor ID.
- Vendor name must match the registered vendor record.
- GSTIN should match the vendor master record where applicable.
- Payment terms should match the registered agreement.
- Vendor bank details must be verified through an authorized process.

## Validation Rules
- Unknown vendor: Send to Vendor Management.
- Vendor name mismatch: Flag for manual review.
- GSTIN mismatch: Flag for review.
- Inactive vendor: Do not automatically approve invoices.

## Routing
Vendor-related issues should be sent to the Vendor Management Department.
