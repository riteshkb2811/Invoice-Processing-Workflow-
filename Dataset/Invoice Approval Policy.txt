# Invoice Approval Policy

## Purpose
To establish approval rules for invoices submitted to the company.

## Approval Rules

- Invoices below ₹10,000: Automatic approval after successful validation.
- Invoices between ₹10,000 and ₹50,000: Department Manager approval required.
- Invoices between ₹50,001 and ₹2,00,000: Finance Manager approval required.
- Invoices above ₹2,00,000: Finance Head approval required.

## Additional Rules
- Invoices with missing mandatory information must be sent for manual review.
- Invoices with suspicious or inconsistent information must not be automatically approved.
- Approval decisions must be recorded in the system.

## Routing
- Department Manager → Department expenses
- Finance Manager → Medium and high-value invoices
- Finance Head → High-value invoices
